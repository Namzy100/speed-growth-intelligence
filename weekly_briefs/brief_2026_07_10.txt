This week reveals three critical findings. First, organic installs now comprise 65 percent of total volume at 29,582 installs with zero paid cost, creating the clearest signal yet that word-of-mouth and product-market fit are driving growth faster than paid media. Second, Apple Search Ads is delivering the lowest paid eCPI at $2.84 overall and an exceptional $2.16 for the US Brand Exact campaign, substantially outperforming Google Ads at $4.48 and Facebook at $3.14. Third, Meta's Cash Deposit campaign is hemorrhaging budget with an eCPI of $106.74 across just 14 installs, converting at a rate seven times worse than the retargeting campaign and thirty-seven times worse than the profitable Payday Android Broad+ creative.

Apple Search Ads is the most efficient paid channel right now. The US Brand Exact campaign alone delivered 2,115 installs at $2.16 per user, indicating that crypto-curious users actively searching for Speed Wallet convert at higher intent and lower friction than those discovered through display or social feeds. The tight keyword targeting and app-store-native placement filter for users already in a download mindset, and the $2.16 eCPI sits 40 percent below Facebook's best performer and 51 percent below Google's brand campaign.

Facebook urgently needs a budget review. The Cash Deposit campaign burned $1,494 to acquire 14 users, a catastrophic outcome that suggests either severe creative mismatch, wrong audience configuration, or platform delivery failure. Even the retargeting and re-engagement campaigns show eCPIs of $18.17 and $24.07 respectively, both well above acceptable thresholds for warm audiences. Meanwhile, the one Facebook winner—Payday Android Broad+—accounts for 87 percent of Meta installs at a healthy $2.87 eCPI. The platform itself isn't broken, but five of six campaigns are.

Immediately pause the Cash Deposit, Re-targeting, and Re-engagement campaigns on Meta and reallocate that combined weekly budget of roughly $580 into the Payday Android Broad+ creative, which is already proving it can scale at under $3 per install with a click-through rate of 7.74 percent. This consolidation will prevent waste while doubling down on the only Meta asset currently delivering profitable volume. Use the next two weeks to audit why Cash Deposit failed so dramatically—whether the creative misrepresented the product, the audience was misconfigured, or the offer itself lacks market appetite—before attempting any similar deposit-focused messaging again.

D1 retention is essentially flat. The most recent seven-day average of matured cohorts sits at 26.6 percent, up just 0.6 percentage points from the prior week's 26.0 percent. This stability suggests that the user experience is consistent and that the flood of organic installs isn't degrading quality, but it also signals that onboarding and day-one activation haven't meaningfully improved despite volume growth. If Speed wants to capitalize on the organic momentum, now is the moment to instrument first-session conversion funnels and test friction-reduction tactics in the deposit or first-transaction flow.

Robinhood is leaning heavily into premium membership perks like Gold APY benefits and advanced trading tools such as the Legend platform, targeting sophisticated active traders rather than beginners, with crypto positioned as just another asset class. Crypto.com is doubling down on gamification and incentive loops—missions, mystery boxes, card cashback—combined with zero-fee promotions, using carousel ads and rewards-based CTAs to drive habitual engagement rather than one-time conversions.

Germany, the United Kingdom, and Portugal emerge as the top three EU entry markets. Germany's 1,657 existing organic installs represent the largest untapped user base Speed hasn't yet activated, and a single EMI license unlocks the entire EU regulatory perimeter from there. The UK offers the highest-volume India and Nigeria remittance corridors in Europe plus the continent's most mature iGaming infrastructure, though it requires separate post-Brexit licensing. Portugal delivers the densest Brazil-corridor concentration per capita, enabling a tightly focused launch among Brazilian nationals that can generate word-of-mouth velocity before broader scaling.
